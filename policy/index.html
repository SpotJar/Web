Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy covers the SpotJar app (iOS and Android) and the website spotjar.app. SpotJar needs no account, and your places, notes, photos and trips stay on your device. Only what you choose to share goes to a server (section 6). A short summary is in the app under Settings, at the info button next to the privacy note.

1. Controller

Elias Rist, Weiler Haus 22a, 6300 Wörgl, Austria

SpotJar is run by a private individual (Imprint). Requests are answered within one month. The GDPR and the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG) apply.

2. Age and voluntary use

SpotJar is meant for people aged 16 and over; age is not checked. You only give up data when you use a feature that needs it. Without it that feature does not work, the rest of the app does. There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

3. Website

The static website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes your IP address, the page requested, the time and browser and system details to deliver and protect the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, secure operation). We do not see these logs and have no statistics. GitHub's privacy statement.

There are no cookies, no analytics or advertising services, no forms and no embedded content from other sites; the fonts are served from the site. Only if you pick a language in the switcher does your browser remember that choice in local storage (spotjar-lang) so the page does not jump back. This is technically necessary for your selection (§ 165(3) TKG 2021) and is sent nowhere. Links to the App Store, Google Play or your email app transfer something only after you click; the provider's policy then applies. Invitation and trip links carry their identifier after the “#”, which browsers never send to the server.

4. Data on your device

Everything you enter is kept in the app's private storage: places (name, address, coordinates, link, notes, categories, photos, markers), trips with plans, accommodations and packing lists, your profile and travel journal, and settings. This is providing the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); we do not receive this data.

  • Sharing to SpotJar: A link shared from another app is handed to the app through a shared folder on the device.
  • Export/import: You save or send the export file (photos only on request) yourself. Instagram and TikTok cover images are excluded even from exports with photos.
  • Trip from photos: Text recognition and analysis of the photos you choose run on the device (section 9). The camera is used only for that, and only after you agree.
  • Device backups: Depending on your device settings, iCloud or Google may back up the app data; that concerns only you and the provider.

You delete single items in the app, and everything by uninstalling it.

6. Account, group trips and trip links

You sign in only for these two features: with Google (iOS, Android) or Apple (iOS), via Firebase Authentication. Data is stored in Cloud Firestore (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA; location EU, eur3). This EU region applies to the Firestore database, not to all Firebase services: according to Google, Firebase Authentication processes sign-in data in the USA; App Check and Cloud Messaging can process globally. Google processes account and trip data supplied by us as a processor. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because you want to share the trip. Firebase App Check (Apple App Attest, Google Play Integrity) verifies that requests come from the genuine app and transmits device attestations to Apple or Google for that (point (f), protection against abuse).

Group trips

  • Account: user ID, name and email address from Google or Apple (Apple may supply a relay address), sign-in provider; plus the name other members see (editable) and the web address of your Google profile picture, which the others' phones load from Google themselves.
  • Trip: name, region (name, coordinates, radius), days, dates, owner, members with roles (organizer, planner, viewer), invite code.
  • Plan: per entry day, order, break name, duration and time, done flag, note, who added it and who ticked it as seen; per place name, coordinates, address, city, country, category, source link, provider ID.
  • Accommodations: check-in and check-out, nights, booking code and link, provider, phone, email, notes, details of the accommodation as for places; never the documents.
  • Photos of places, accommodations and the cover: your own photos only as a preview (JPEG, at most 800 pixels, re-encoded on the device, so without camera or location data); free Wikimedia photos only as a link that each phone loads from Wikimedia itself (without coordinates or place names).

Visible only to members, that is, whoever signed in and enters the code or opens the invite link (https://spotjar.app/j/#…); anyone who has the link or code can join. Before joining, a preview shows name, region, length, dates and the name of whoever shared it. Firebase Security Rules enforce this on the server. Whoever joins starts as a viewer. Never uploaded are your other places, notes, favorite and experienced markers, packing lists, booking documents, original photos, settings and your location.

Ending: When you leave, your name and ID are removed from the trip and it is deleted from your device; the last member deletes it from the server. The owner can dissolve the group (trip, plan, photos and invite are deleted at once, and the owner may keep a private copy); organizers can remove members. Those affected get a notice (their ID, the trip's ID and name, dissolved or removed, the name of who did it) that only they can read and that is deleted once shown. So that you can keep the plan afterwards, each phone also stores it locally. Under Settings › Account you can delete your account: you leave all groups (you dissolve ones you own), and your Firebase account, push data and preferences are deleted; entries you added stay without your name. Signing out deletes nothing on the server.

Trip by link

You can share a private trip as a link (https://spotjar.app/t/#…); you must be signed in. It is stored in Cloud Firestore for 24 hours: name, region, dates, plan with breaks, places (as above), the custom categories they use, photos as previews (a Wikimedia photo with its credit), cover and your account ID; never notes, markers, accommodations or the packing list. During that time anyone with the link can open it without an account and import it as their own copy. After that the server refuses access, and your app deletes the link at its next start. Shared as a file (.spotjartrip), the trip contains the same and goes only where you send it.

7. Notifications

For group trips SpotJar can send push notifications: a summary of new places (15 minutes after a person last added one), a changed role and removal from a group. The app asks for permission only when you open a group or switch a kind on in Settings. Each kind has its own switch in Settings, and each group can additionally be muted. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, system dialog); you can withdraw it at any time.

Stored in Firestore, readable only by your account: your device's push token with a Firebase installation ID and the app language, your switches, mutes and which places you have already seen in an opened plan (so you get no notification about known things). Delivery runs through Cloud Functions and Cloud Tasks in europe-west1 and through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push service (APNs); they process the group name, the name of the person who added, the number of new places and the trip ID, no coordinates or photos. Markers that prevent duplicate notifications (IDs, timestamps) are deleted after 30 days. Signing out removes the token; deleting the account removes all of it.

8. Error reports

The app can send crash and error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., 45 Fremont Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA; processed in the EU region, as processor) to fix bugs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). A report contains the error with its technical trace, the latest technical log messages (internal identifiers, no names or notes; rarely a link that could not be processed), device model, operating system, app version and your IP address. No screenshots, no performance measurement, no link to your account. You can switch it off under Settings, Send error reports; the app then sends nothing and does not start Sentry. Sentry's privacy policy.

9. AI features

SpotJar uses only the AI that your operating system provides (Apple Foundation Models, Gemini Nano through Android AICore), and only on the device. Texts, photos and results go neither to us nor to third parties or a cloud AI. It is used for suggestions: a trip from photos of a travel plan (iOS), place names in shared TikTok and Instagram posts, and titles and descriptions of trip ideas. Text recognition (Apple Vision, ML Kit from Google Play services) also reads text on photos and cover pictures on the device. Everything an AI produced carries this mark:

These are unchecked suggestions that can be wrong, especially opening hours, distances, prices and access conditions. Check them yourself before you travel. Nothing is saved without your confirmation. You can switch them off under Settings, AI features.

10. Transfers to third countries

Requests described in this policy may involve processing outside the EEA, particularly in the USA. An EU database or EU region does not rule out access or further processing in third countries across all services. Such transfers must satisfy Articles 44 onwards GDPR; the relevant grounds depend on the specific recipient, service and applicable agreements.

Providers publish information on privacy and transfers at Google/Firebase and in Sentry's data processing terms; other recipients are linked in sections 3 and 5. Provider information does not replace any required agreement. You can use the contact details in section 1 to request information about the safeguards used for a transfer and, where applicable, a copy of those safeguards.

11. Retention

  • On the device: until you delete it in the app or uninstall. The trash keeps deleted places and trips for 14 days.
  • Account: until you delete it under Settings › Account.
  • Group trip: until the last member leaves or the owner deletes or dissolves it; replaced photos at once, photos of removed places with the place.
  • Trip link: 24 hours.
  • Push data: until you sign out, leave the group or delete the account; markers 30 days.
  • Error reports: according to the retention setting of the Sentry project. You can request the current period using the contact details in section 1.
  • Logs at GitHub, Apple, Google, Wikimedia, Meta, TikTok, OpenStreetMap servers, Geoapify and komoot: under their policies; we have no access.

12. Your rights

You have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), data portability (20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interests (21), and to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)), for example for location or notifications in your device settings. Since most data stays on your device, you exercise many rights directly in the app (deleting, export, leaving groups, deleting the account). Otherwise write to the address in section 1.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at, or in the EU state where you live or work.

13. Changes and contact

If the app or website changes in a way that affects your data, we update this policy; the current version with its date is always here. Questions and privacy requests: app.spotjar@gmail.com.
