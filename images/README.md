# Website image provenance

Recorded on 2026-10-29: the project owner states that the illustrations were created by them,
partly with ChatGPT assistance. No unrelated stock images were added during the redesign.
This records the owner's statement; it is not independent clearance of every depicted element.

- `img/illustrations/*.webp` are optimized copies of artwork in the sibling app repository's
  `assets/images/illustrations/`; `img/illustrations/spotjar-full.svg` and `img/marker-*.svg`
  use the SVG originals from that repository. The source mapping is in the website README.
- The app screenshots in `img/app-*` come from SpotJar. Map data, third-party pictures, logos and
  interfaces depicted inside a screenshot or illustration can have separate rights and usage
  conditions. Preserve visible provider attributions; review promotional usage independently.
- The wordmark and other SpotJar artwork are supplied by the project owner.
- App Store and Google Play badges remain third-party brand assets and retain their providers'
  conditions. Fredoka and Inter remain under the font licences in `fonts/`.

Keep original artwork and creation records. Do not treat AI assistance or possession of an image
file as proof that a depicted third-party photograph or map was licensed for every use.
