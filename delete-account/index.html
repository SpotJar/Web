Delete your SpotJar account

Last updated 30 October 2026

You can delete your SpotJar account and its associated account data yourself directly in the app. If needed, you can also request deletion by email, for example if you have already uninstalled the app. SpotJar is operated by Elias Rist.

Delete directly in the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm deletion. You may need to sign in again with Google or Apple. Export anything you want to keep before deleting.

For more information, read our privacy policy.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot delete your account yourself in the app, you can email us.

Open a deletion request email

Or write directly to app.spotjar@gmail.com with the subject “Delete SpotJar account”.

  1. Include your SpotJar account email address and whether you signed in with Google or Apple. For Apple, this may be a Hide My Email relay address.
  2. Say that you want your SpotJar account and its associated data deleted. The request must be sent from the email address registered with your SpotJar account. This confirms that you have access to that email address. Simply mentioning the address in the message is not enough, as we otherwise cannot verify that it belongs to you.
  3. We handle requests manually and confirm deletion. If needed, we will ask you to confirm that the account belongs to you. You will receive a response within one month.

You do not need to sign in to this website or reinstall the app. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Which data is deleted?

  • Your SpotJar sign-in account in Firebase Authentication, including its user ID, name, email address and sign-in provider.
  • Your group memberships, displayed name and profile picture data, plus your account's push tokens, notification preferences and read markers.
  • Deleting your account in the app dissolves groups you own: their shared plan, bookings, photos and invitations are removed from the server. Other members lose access. Groups owned by other people remain.

This deletes your SpotJar account. Your Google or Apple account remains. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete your SpotJar account.

What may remain, and for how long?

  • Shared content: Contributions and photos in groups owned by others may remain until that content is removed or the group is dissolved. Your name is removed from the members list; technical author references may remain on existing contributions. Please also identify personal content you want removed in your deletion request.
  • Trip links: Links can be opened for 24 hours after creation. Expired server copies are normally cleaned up the next time your signed-in app starts. For email deletion requests, we include any remaining trip links associated with your account in our review.
  • Technical notification markers: IDs and timestamps used to prevent duplicate messages are scheduled for automatic deletion after 30 days.
  • Local data and copies: Account deletion does not automatically remove private places, photos, trips, packing lists, exports or device backups from your devices or copies already saved by other people. Delete local app data in the app or by removing the app together with its data; manage exported files and backups where you saved them.

Error reports and connection logs are processed independently of your SpotJar account. Their retention periods and contact details for further deletion requests are explained in the privacy policy.
